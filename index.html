    Heritage & Lineage

    Master Craftsman Alangara Mathiyazhagan

    Born into the profound temple sculpting heritage of Tamil Nadu, Alangara Mathiyazhagan established Mathi Kamala Arts to solve a perennial temple dilemma: the vulnerability and immense weight of granite and cast panchaloha murtis during procession.

    Employing aerospace-grade fibreglass matrices reinforced with core mineral powders, our atelier yields deity sculptures that resist solar UV degradation, tolerate daily milk and water abhishekams without color bleed, and preserve holy Shilpa Shastra proportions for generations.
